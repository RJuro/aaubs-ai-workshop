# 04 A short paper in LaTeX

**Input:**
- `input/notes.md`: notes for a short paper: question, data, what goes in each section, style.
- `input/municipalities_2024.csv`: the 98 municipalities with income 2023 and 2024, population, and the share with a master's degree or PhD (Statistics Denmark).
- `input/references.bib`: the five references it may cite.
- `input/template/`: a usual setup (`main.tex`, `preamble.tex`, `sections/`).

**Prompt:**
> Work in `workflows/04-latex-paper`. Write the short paper from my notes, with the figure and the regression, and compile it.

**Skills it reads:** `notebook`, `latex-paper`. Needs TeX (`latexmk`).

**Then try:** "make the introduction half as long"; your own notes and data.
