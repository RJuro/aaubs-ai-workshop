# Review protocol: generative AI and productivity at work

Written with the user before the search. Changing this file and replaying the run gives a new, documented review.

## Question

What does recent empirical research find about the effect of generative AI tools on the productivity of workers or firms?

## Search

arXiv, categories econ.GN, econ.EM, cs.CY, cs.HC; "generative AI" or "large language model" or ChatGPT, together with productivity, workers or firms. 60 most relevant records.

## Inclusion criteria (all must hold)

- C1 Empirical: the paper analyses data it collected or obtained (experiment, field study, survey, administrative or firm data). Not a review, essay, framework or simulation only.
- C2 Generative AI: the tool studied is generative AI (LLMs, ChatGPT, Copilot, image generators). Not older AI or automation in general.
- C3 Work outcome: it measures an outcome of work: productivity, output, quality, speed, earnings, employment or task performance of workers, teams or firms.

Decision "unsure" when the abstract does not say enough. Unsure records go to a person.

## Extraction (full text, each with a verbatim quote from the paper)

- setting: country, sector or occupation
- design: experiment, field experiment, observational, survey
- sample: who and how many
- ai_tool: which tool and how it was used
- outcome: what was measured
- finding: direction and size of the main effect
- caveat: the main limitation the authors state
