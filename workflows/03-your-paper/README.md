# 03 Your own paper, to read and to hear

**Input:** a paper you wrote yourself (PDF), so there are no copyright questions. Put it in `input/`.

**Prompt:**
> Work in `workflows/03-your-paper`. Turn my paper into a clean, readable one-column PDF and a one-minute audio summary with a Mistral voice.

**Skills it reads:** `notebook`, `ocr`, `tts`, `mistral-services`. The PDF needs TeX (`latexmk`).

**Then try:** a 6-slide AAU deck from the paper (skill `paper-to-deck`); a summary for a non-academic reader; "check that every number in the paper survived".
