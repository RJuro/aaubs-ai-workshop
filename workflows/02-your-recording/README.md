# 02 Your own recording

**Input:** a recording you make yourself: a voice memo on your phone (two minutes of you explaining a research idea is enough), or a meeting where everyone agreed to be recorded. Put the file (m4a, mp3, wav, mp4) in `input/`.

**Prompt:**
> Work in `workflows/02-your-recording`. Transcribe my recording with Mistral, name the speakers, and give me a short summary with the main points and their timestamps.

**Skills it reads:** `notebook`, `transcribe`, `mistral-services`.

**Then try:** "pull out every open question and who raised it"; "turn it into minutes with action points"; "read the summary aloud with a Mistral voice" (skill `tts`).

Recordings of other people are personal data: only with their consent, and not in AAU's default Mistral workspace. Your own voice memo about your own work is fine.
